To recreate the generated part of glidekvm-client manpage use a command like:
  help2man -N --output=glidekvm-client.1 --name='GlideKVM Keyboard/Mouse Client' /path/to/glidekvm-client

And for glidekvm-server:
  help2man -N --output=glidekvm-server.1 --name='GlideKVM Keyboard/Mouse Server' /path/to/glidekvm-server

After that manually fixup:
 * replace your home directory with $HOME in glidekvm-server.1
 * revert formatting of FILES and COPYRIGHT sections to match formatting of existing version.
 * add note referencing this file for instructions to the first line.
